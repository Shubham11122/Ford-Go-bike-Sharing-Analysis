# 🚴 Ford GoBike Trip Analysis

> A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on the Ford GoBike 2018 trip dataset using Python to uncover customer behavior, ride patterns, station utilization, and business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Bike-sharing systems generate millions of trip records that can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and operational efficiency. This project analyzes the **Ford GoBike 2018 Trip Dataset** to understand riding patterns, customer demographics, trip characteristics, and station performance.

The project follows a complete data analysis workflow, including:

- Data Understanding
- Data Quality Assessment
- Data Cleaning
- Feature Engineering
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Business Insights
- Business Recommendations

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and riding behavior.
- Identify peak ride periods across months, weekdays, and hours.
- Study trip duration patterns.
- Discover the busiest stations and popular travel routes.
- Evaluate bike utilization.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Subscribers are the dominant customer segment.
- Riders aged 26–35 represent the largest user group.
- Weekday commuting drives the majority of trips.
- Morning (8 AM) and evening (5 PM) are peak demand periods.
- Most trips last between 5–10 minutes.
- San Francisco Caltrain Station 2 is the busiest station.
- Bike-sharing primarily supports short-distance urban commuting.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Optimize bike availability during peak commuting hours.
